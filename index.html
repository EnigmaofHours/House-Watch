Everything you tick is stored in this browser only, and opening the link on another device starts fresh. The single exception is weather: the page asks Open-Meteo and the National Weather Service about the ZIP code you set in Setup, every quarter hour. Nothing about you or your device goes with that question, and the board works without it. The indicator at the top says whether saving is working; if it reads Not saving, tap it, and treat anything you enter after that as good for this visit only. Dates are calculated in the house's time zone, not yours.
